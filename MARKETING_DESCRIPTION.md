# FamilyStyle App Store Description

With FamilyStyle splitting restaurant bills has never been easier.

Key Features:
*   **No App Needed:** Send totals and Venmo links via text. Friends can pay through Venmo.
*   **Flexible Splitting:** Tap a name once to assign an item, or multiple times if they're paying for others.
*   **Smart Scan:** Multiple quantities are auto-split into individual items for easy assignment.
*   **Total Control:** Every price is editable, and you can reference the original receipt on any screen.
*   **Accurate Splits:** We calculate tax and tip to the penny.
*   **Track Your Tabs:** Keep a complete history of past bills and view fun insights like your total spending and most visited restaurants.
*   **Privacy First:** No accounts required and no cloud storage. All your data stays strictly on your device.

Pass the plate, not the bill. FamilyStyle handles the rest!
